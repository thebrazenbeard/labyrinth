## Unpassed gates and next falsifiable experiment

- No uniform-versus-adaptive trained comparison, multi-seed training, continual-learning retention, or cross-family transfer test.
- The sham has equal case visits and optimizer calls but intentionally no backward gradients; it is an intervention control, not equal compute time.
- The two-step intervention is too small to meaningfully test learning capacity. Do not tune on final seed 9703 or reuse it as a fresh final after inspecting outcomes.
