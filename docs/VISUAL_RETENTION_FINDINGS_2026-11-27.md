# Visual witness retention: marginal benefit versus acquisition cost

Status: **offline procedural-policy development evidence**, not model training, a qualified external benchmark, or an installed runtime change. 2026-11-27.

## Question and setup

Does a retained search priority outperform a reset policy after circumstances change, when feedback must come from the already-budgeted inspection?

The task is to return an exact changed-pixel witness or identify unchanged bytes, otherwise abstain. It is not segmentation, semantic perception or complete localization. Common observations were generated by Vision at `eda87da062a22f52dcf06cab6e17f4b55fddd4a6` (frames.py blob `3be67d64c66a2b383b876ea7fa6223faf7ec7987`, temporal.py blob `ddb9a617a0f9d6b697f719d693db5f62f05f09b9`). Synthetic RGB64x64 pairs: 12 paired seeds, 160 cases per seed, four layout families, six policies. Each pair receives at most 1024 additional pixel comparisons; common digest/sample cost and policy bookkeeping are excluded. Matching digests are practical equality evidence, not mathematical proof of byte identity. Generated truth is not provided to the policy. A witness updates preference; rotation policies also advance after abstention. The prior preferred quadrant was acquired in a separate 160-case workload.

## Observed development results

Correct resolutions out of 1920 cases per family. All other outcomes were abstentions; zero wrong outcomes in these fixtures. Cold continuation and normal/optimized Python agreed on non-timing records across all 46,080 outcomes.

| Policy | Stable | Abrupt | Alternating | Uniform |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Row-major | 1155 | 673 | 578 | 677 |
| Retained priority | 1920 | 1056 | 960 | 625 |
| Witness update, retained | 1920 | 1056 | 960 | 625 |
| Witness update, reset | 1908 | 1044 | 950 | 611 |
| Rotation, retained | 1920 | 1908 | 372 | 618 |
| Rotation, reset | 1908 | 1896 | 372 | 603 |

A fixed prior beats row-major in aligned layouts, but loses in the uniform family. Rotation helps abrupt drift and harms alternating layouts. No universal selector is supported.

## Paired cost audit

The original prior acquisition replay cost **142,035 additional pixel comparisons**, excluded from the above marginal-use cap. A fresh audit of the retained per-case records computes paired wins/losses (resolved by only one arm) and net comparison savings relative to the comparator. Positive savings mean the retained arm used fewer comparisons.

| Family | Retained rotation vs reset rotation wins/losses | Net saved | Acquisition/saved per batch |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stable | 12/0 | 6197 | 22.92 |
| Abrupt | 12/0 | 6197 | 22.92 |
| Alternating | 180/180 | -534 | no break-even |
| Uniform | 32/17 | 7383 | 19.24 |

This is an arithmetic amortization illustration, **not a forecast**: it assumes future batches repeat the same marginal savings. It ignores differences in the value of resolutions, acquisition opportunity cost, digest/sampling cost, and runtime overhead. When knowledge already exists for independent reasons, acquisition may be sunk; when acquired specifically for this task, it must be included. The reset comparator is essential: attributing the entire rotation gain to retention would be misleading. In the alternating family equal aggregate accuracy hides 360 discordant paired outcomes.

Recompute by joining records on family, seed, case index and comparing status and comparison counts; verify equal key sets, bounds and non-timing equality between normal and optimized runs. For example stable retained rotation uses 798527 comparisons versus reset 804724; 804724 - 798527 = 6197. Divide 142035 by 6197 to obtain the illustrative batch count.

## Admission recommendation

Retain the findings, not a compulsory adaptive policy. Prefer exact comparison when the resource budget permits. Preserve UNKNOWN rather than inferring no change from sampled static evidence. Before a retained procedure is operationally preferred, compare with a reset version of the *same* adaptive policy, charge prior acquisition when relevant, and account for per-family failures and abstentions. A persisted state or successful serialization is not proof of competence. A changed task may render a valid retained method inapplicable without erasing it.

For independent qualification, reserve independently authored layout families and frozen success criteria; charge lifetime acquisition, observations, computation, feedback and interruption/recovery costs; compare matched budgets and paired outcomes. No model weights, reward mechanisms or runtime settings are modified by this note.
